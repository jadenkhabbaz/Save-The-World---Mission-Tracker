THE COMMANDER'S FIELD GUIDE

Save the World, at a glance

A quick guide to mission difficulty, enemies and the upgrades that make your loadout stronger.

01 / MISSIONS

How Save the World works

Save the World is Fortnite's co-op campaign. Team up, build defenses and protect objectives from Husks during missions.

02 / ENEMIES

Know the threats

Husks attack in groups. Mist Monsters are tougher special enemies, while Mini-Bosses bring extra challenges to selected missions.

03 / POWER

Power Level

Your Power Level reflects your overall strength. Heroes, Survivor Squads, research and F.O.R.T. attributes all help shape it.

04 / LOADOUT

Heroes & Schematics

Heroes provide abilities and perks. Survivor Squads boost your stats, while weapon and Trap Schematics let you craft and upgrade gear.

05 / PROGRESSION

Build your way through the campaign

Progress through Stonewood, Plankerton, Canny Valley and Twine Peaks by completing quests and Storm Shield defenses. Evolve eligible Heroes, Survivors and Schematics as you unlock stronger upgrades.

Before you launch

Check the mission reward, zone and recommended Power Level. If tracker data differs from the game, trust the in-game mission screen.
