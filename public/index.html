01 / THE BASICS

What is Save the World?

Save the World is Fortnite's cooperative player-versus-environment campaign. You take command of a Hero, work with the Homebase crew, rescue Survivors and defend objectives from creatures emerging with the Storm. Missions often ask your squad to build and protect an objective while fighting off waves of enemies.

02 / THREATS

Know your enemies

Husks are the common attackers and usually arrive in groups. Mist Monsters are more dangerous special enemies with stronger attacks or abilities. Mini-Bosses are tougher mission threats; major story encounters include the Storm King.

03 / POWER

Power Level & F.O.R.T.

Your Power Level is a broad indicator of your combat strength and helps determine which mission difficulties are suitable. It is influenced by your F.O.R.T. attributes: Fortitude, Offense, Resistance and Tech. Heroes, Survivors and research progression all contribute to your overall build.

04 / YOUR COLLECTION

Heroes, Survivors & Schematics

Your Commander Hero leads the loadout, supported by additional Heroes whose perks strengthen the build. Survivor Squads contribute to your stats. Weapon and Trap Schematics let you craft equipment, improve its perks and evolve it as you progress.

05 / CRAFTING

Evolution & upgrades

As you advance, you can evolve eligible Heroes, Survivors, Schematics and Traps using the required materials. Higher evolution tiers unlock stronger versions. Some weapon paths offer different crafting materials and trade-offs, so check the schematic details before investing resources.

06 / THE CAMPAIGN

From Stonewood to Twine Peaks

The main campaign moves through Stonewood, Plankerton, Canny Valley and Twine Peaks. Completing quests and Storm Shield objectives opens more of the world and higher-level content. Ventures and rotating event questlines add separate seasonal challenges.

07 / THE STORY

Homebase, Ray & the Storm

The campaign begins after a catastrophic Storm leaves a small group of survivors fighting to rebuild. Homebase becomes the centre of the resistance, with the Commander working alongside Ray and the rest of the crew to establish safe zones, defend communities and uncover what is driving the Storm. The story unfolds through campaign missions and event questlines.

Quick mission checklist

Check the reward, region and Power Level before launching. Mission Alerts rotate, and tracker updates can occasionally lag behind the game. If anything conflicts, the in-game mission screen is the final source of truth.
